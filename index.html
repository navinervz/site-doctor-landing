    Questions

    Is it really free?

    Yes. The health check and the emailed report are free, with no card. If you want help fixing what we find, you can talk to a specialist, but you never have to.

    What do you actually check?

    The real page and server: indexing (robots, sitemap, canonical, noindex), on-page SEO, HTTPS and security headers, speed with Google PageSpeed and Core Web Vitals, and email authentication (SPF, DMARC).

    Does AI make up the problems?

    No. Problems come from measurements on your live site. AI only explains them in plain language and orders the plan. It cannot add findings of its own.

    What happens to my email?

    We use it to send your report and to limit abuse. We do not sell it and we do not subscribe you to newsletters.
